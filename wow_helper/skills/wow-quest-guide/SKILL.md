name: wow-quest-guide
description: Suggest quest priorities from a WoW Companion snapshot and explain a selected quest, with attention to the selected game edition and snapshot age.
---

Use the quest report supplied with the request. State its age; it updates on the player's reload or logout, not continuously. Distinguish reported objectives from advice. Favor turn-ins, nearby doable objectives, and existing progress; explain when a group or high level makes a quest unsuitable.

Classic Forever beta may differ from Classic or Retail. Verify edition-specific facts when browsing is available; link to the source and identify uncertainty when it is not. An empty available-quest list does not establish that no quests exist nearby. Do not invent routes or coordinates from a quest title alone.

Answer the requested planning question with brief, actionable advice for the player. This workflow does not authorize game input, accepting quests, or changing the quest log.
