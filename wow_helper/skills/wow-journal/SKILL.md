---
name: wow-journal
description: Summarize the player's selected local journal notes and identify goals to resume, keeping recorded events separate from suggestions.
---

Use the dated entries supplied in the preparation bundle. Preserve unresolved goals and explicit user corrections. Do not claim an event happened merely because an earlier entry planned it. Call out conflicting entries when the difference matters to the next action.

Summarize progress and suggest a useful next goal in a short response. If the journal lacks enough information, identify the missing detail. The reply is a draft summary; the player chooses whether to add it to the journal. Do not edit the journal file, infer character identity, or send game input.
